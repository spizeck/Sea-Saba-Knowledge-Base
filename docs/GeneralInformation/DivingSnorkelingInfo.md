## For Those Not Certified

!!! warning

### Scuba Discovery
  - An option for trying scuba diving for uncertified guests
  - Cost: $170 per person (includes transfers, gear, instruction, and afternoon dive)
  - Schedule: Pickup at 8:30 am **(Bring a Lunch)**, gear fitting, confined water session, afternoon dive at 1:00 pm. Back to the harbor by approximately 3:00 pm

  - Maximum ratio: 2 students per instructor

### Open Water Course (3-Day Full Certification)
  - Online instruction (about 20 hrs), must be completed before in water sessions
  - Cost: $749 per person (includes transfers, gear, all fees and taxes)
  - Pickup at 8:30 am **(Bring a Lunch)**
  - We will update pickup times after your first day

## Multi-Day Discounts

- Multi-day discounts are built into the tiered pricing above
- Discounts are designed for guests diving on a mostly continuous schedule — equipment staging and logistics are optimized for continuous diving
- We allow **one rest day** between dive days without resetting the discount
- Taking more than one day off requires re-staging equipment and the **multi-day discount resets** — the gap is treated as a new booking

## Things You Might See Underwater

Marine life sightings are never guaranteed, but Saba's protected marine park is home to a healthy diversity of species.

  - Turtles (Green and Hawksbill)
  - Sharks (Nurse Sharks, Reef Sharks)
    - Occasional sightings: Hammerheads and Tiger Sharks
  - Various fish (see the map for common species)
  - Nudibranchs
  - Whales are seasonal and uncommon, but possible during winter months — you may hear them underwater, and surface sightings occasionally occur on the way to dive sites or during surface intervals

## Boat and Additional Information
  - Water and Gatorade are provided on the boat
  - Guests should bring: towels, reef-safe sunscreen, swimwear, and snacks or lunch where noted
  - Reusable water bottles are welcome
  - On-board restroom available primarily for emergencies due to vessel size and operational limitations
  - Avoid hiking Mt. Scenery after diving
  - Visit our website for more information and pictures: www.seasaba.com

## Supervision Ratios & Group Sizes

- **Guided recreational dives**: maximum 8 divers per guide
- **Standard training courses**: maximum 4 students per instructor
- **Scuba Discovery**: maximum 2 students per instructor

Ratios may be reduced further depending on weather, visibility, currents, diver experience, or operational judgment. Safety and guest comfort take priority over maximizing group size.

> Staff should never promise private guiding or larger ratios than the above without prior operational approval.

## Operational Notes

- Dive sites are selected based on weather, currents, visibility, diver experience, and overall conditions on the day
- Site requests are always welcome and will be considered, but cannot be guaranteed
- Safety decisions are made by the captain and dive staff based on conditions at the time
- When in doubt about a booking — certifications, inactive divers, course scheduling, or group size — call the Harbor (ph# 3544) before confirming
