# Business Travel & Per Diem Policy

## Purpose

To establish clear expectations for employees traveling on behalf of Sea Saba, ensure that all reasonable business-related travel expenses are reimbursed fairly, and prevent confusion about schedules, responsibilities, and compensation during travel days.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all Sea Saba employees and managers who travel for business purposes, including inter-island trips, supply runs, equipment service trips, training, and any other company-directed travel.

## General Travel Expectations

1. **Pre-Trip Discussion**: Before any business trip, the employee and their manager should discuss and agree on travel expectations, including schedule, responsibilities, reimbursements, and per diem arrangements. Both parties should have a clear understanding before departure.
2. **Professional Conduct**: Employees represent Sea Saba while traveling. Professional and respectful behavior is expected at all times, including during downtime.
3. **Reachability**: Employees must remain reachable during business travel and be prepared to resume work responsibilities as needed.
4. **Lodging**: The company will always provide and pay for housing and lodging for approved overnight business travel. Employees should not need to cover accommodation costs out of pocket.

## Expense Reimbursement

The company will reimburse all reasonable and authorized business-related travel expenses, including:

- **Transportation**: Ferries, taxis, fuel, tolls, and parking fees.
- **Supplies & Materials**: Job-related purchases approved in advance.
- **Other Authorized Costs**: Any expense directly required to complete the business purpose of the trip.

> Retain receipts whenever reasonably possible, especially for transportation and larger purchases. Receipts help ensure timely and accurate reimbursement.

!!! note
    Any unusual or large expenses should be discussed with and approved by management before being incurred. When in doubt, ask first.

## Per Diem Policy

Employees traveling for business are entitled to a **food per diem of $80 USD per full travel day**. Per diem is paid in addition to the employee's regular daily wages unless otherwise specified by management before the trip.

### Standard Per Diem Breakdown

| Meal | Amount |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | $15 |
| Lunch | $30 |
| Dinner | $35 |
| **Total** | **$80** |

> The lunch allocation is intended to cover the full midday period, including snacks, beverages, and bottled water purchased throughout the day.

### Per Diem Adjustments

Management may adjust the per diem amount or structure based on the following:

- **Trip length or destination**: Day trips, short runs, or local errands may receive a partial per diem reflecting only the meals applicable to that trip.
- **Meals provided directly**: If a manager is present and chooses to pay for meals directly, per diem may not be issued for those meals.
- **Operational arrangements**: In situations where meals are otherwise covered by the company, per diem may be reduced accordingly.

All adjustments will be communicated to the employee in advance of the trip.

## Manager Approval & Communication

1. **Pre-Approval**: All business travel must be approved by management before departure.
2. **Advance Notice**: Where possible, travel plans should be confirmed at least one day in advance so logistics, per diem, and schedule can be arranged.
3. **Clarity Before Departure**: If an employee is uncertain about per diem arrangements, reimbursement expectations, or what is covered, they should ask management before the trip — not after.

## Company-Paid Expenses

The following are always covered directly by the company and are **not** deducted from per diem:

- Lodging and accommodation for overnight trips
- Official vessel fuel and operating costs
- Immigration, customs, or clearance fees related to company operations
- Equipment, parts, or materials purchased for business purposes

## Employee Responsibilities

1. **Communicate**: Report any unexpected expenses, delays, or issues to management promptly.
2. **Stay on Schedule**: Adhere to agreed-upon timelines. If delays occur, notify management as soon as possible.
3. **Handle Required Duties**: Complete all required formalities — including customs, immigration, or vessel check-in/check-out — as part of the travel day's responsibilities.
4. **Spend Responsibly**: Per diem is intended to cover normal, reasonable meal expenses. Employees are expected to use it accordingly.
5. **Submit Receipts**: Provide receipts for reimbursable expenses in a timely manner after returning.

## Example Travel Scenario

**Sint Maarten Yamaha Boat Service Run**

This is a common day-trip example that illustrates how travel expectations and per diem typically work in practice.

### Schedule & Responsibilities

1. **Depart Saba**: Depart in time to arrive at the Sint Maarten bridge before the **9:30 AM opening**. Punctuality is critical — missing the bridge opening will delay the entire service appointment.
2. **Deliver the Boat**: Bring the vessel to Yamaha for scheduled service upon arrival.
3. **Customs & Immigration**: Complete all required customs and immigration check-in procedures on arrival in Sint Maarten.
4. **Free Time**: Once the boat has been delivered and immediate responsibilities are complete, staff are generally free until the boat is ready for pickup. During this time, staff may shop, eat, run personal errands, or relax — while remaining reachable by phone.
5. **Boat Pickup & Departure Prep**: Retrieve the vessel from Yamaha once service is complete.
6. **Customs & Immigration Out**: Complete check-out formalities before departing Sint Maarten.
7. **Depart Sint Maarten**: Be prepared to depart through the bridge at the **4:00 PM opening** to return to Saba.

!!! warning
    Staff must plan around both the 9:30 AM and 4:00 PM bridge openings. Missing the afternoon opening will result in a significantly delayed return.

### Per Diem for This Trip

For a standard Sint Maarten day trip of this type, employees receive:

- **$30 per diem** — covering lunch and incidentals for the day
- **Regular day's wages** — paid as normal

> Breakfast and dinner are not included in the per diem for this trip because employees depart and return within normal working hours. The $30 allocation covers the midday period in Sint Maarten.

## Exceptions & Special Circumstances

Management retains the right to adjust per diem, reimbursement arrangements, or travel expectations in the following situations:

- Trips that involve unusual destinations, extended durations, or significant logistical complexity
- Situations where meals are directly provided by the company or a third party
- Multi-day trips where lodging, meals, or other costs are fully covered
- Emergency or unplanned travel where standard procedures cannot be followed in advance

Any exceptions will be communicated clearly before or as early as possible during the trip.

## Accountability

All employees are responsible for understanding and following this policy. Managers are responsible for communicating per diem arrangements, schedules, and reimbursement expectations clearly before any business trip takes place.

---

> These policies may be updated periodically as the business evolves. If you have any questions about travel expectations, per diem, or what expenses are covered for a specific trip, ask management before you travel.
