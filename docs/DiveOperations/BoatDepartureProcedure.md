# Boat Departure Procedure (Dock and Mooring)

## Purpose

To ensure the vessel is fully prepared before departure and to establish clear communication between captain and crew, preventing premature line release, vessel collisions, diver safety incidents, and equipment loss.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all Sea Saba vessels and all departures from docks, moorings, and temporary tie-ups.

## Responsibilities

### Captain

- **Final Authority**: Has sole authority over vessel movement and departure decisions.
- **Final Verification**: Conducts final visual and operational readiness verification.
- **Command Authority**: Gives the explicit command to release lines—no line shall be released without this direct order.

### Crew

- **Checklist Completion**: Completes the full departure checklist before reporting readiness.
- **Readiness Report**: Reports vessel readiness to the captain using the formal challenge-and-response system.
- **Line Control**: Does not release any dock line, mooring line, or anchor line until instructed by the captain.

## Procedure

### Departure Readiness Checklist

Before departure, verify the following:

#### Divers and Passengers

- **Roll Call**: All divers and passengers accounted for.
- **Guest Safety**: No guests in unsafe positions (e.g., near lines, on gunwales, obstructing pathways).

#### Dive Equipment

- **Tanks**: All tanks secured in racks or designated storage.
- **Gear Bags**: All gear bags secured and stowed properly.
- **Loose Equipment**: No loose equipment on deck that could shift or fall overboard.
- **Camera Equipment**: All camera equipment secured in protective bags or cases.

#### Vessel Equipment

- **Dive Flag**: Lowered and securely stowed.
- **Ladder**: Raised and locked in the secure position.
- **Current Line**: Recovered and properly stowed; no current line deployed in the water.
- **Trailing Lines**: No dock lines, mooring lines, or other lines trailing in the water.
- **Mooring Line**: Prepared for release (if departing from mooring).
- **Engines**: Operating normally; helm station ready.

#### Surroundings

- **Traffic Check**: Area around vessel checked for other boats, swimmers, or obstacles.
- **Conditions Assessed**: Wind and current direction and strength evaluated.
- **Departure Path**: Safe departure path identified and clear.

### Captain-Crew Communication Procedure

Use a formal challenge-and-response system for all departures.

#### Standard Exchange

**Crew**: "Boat ready for departure."

**Captain**: Performs final visual confirmation of checklist items and surroundings.

**Captain**: "Release lines."

**Crew**: "Releasing lines."

!!! danger
    Only after the captain gives the explicit command "Release lines" may dock lines or mooring lines be released. No exceptions.

### Dock Departure Procedure

Follow this sequence for all dock departures:

1. **Complete Checklist**: Crew completes the full Departure Readiness Checklist.
2. **Report Ready**: Crew reports "Boat ready for departure" to the captain.
3. **Captain Confirms**: Captain confirms engines are in gear-ready condition.
4. **Release Command**: Captain gives the command: "Release lines."
5. **Release Lines**: Crew releases lines in the order directed by the captain.
6. **Lines Aboard**: Crew reports "All lines aboard" once all dock lines are fully retrieved and secured.
7. **Depart**: Vessel departs when captain confirms all clear.

### Mooring Departure Procedure

Follow this sequence for all mooring departures:

1. **Complete Checklist**: Crew completes the full Departure Readiness Checklist.
2. **Ladder Up**: Confirm ladder is raised and secured.
3. **Current Line Recovered**: Confirm current line is fully retrieved and stowed.
4. **No Trailing Lines**: Confirm no lines are trailing in the water.
5. **Report Ready**: Crew reports "Boat ready for departure" to the captain.
6. **Captain Confirms**: Captain confirms readiness and safe conditions.
7. **Release Command**: Captain commands "Release mooring."
8. **Retrieve Mooring**: Crew releases and retrieves the mooring line aboard.
9. **Mooring Clear**: Crew reports "Mooring clear" once the line is fully aboard and secured.
10. **Depart**: Vessel departs when captain confirms all clear.

### Stop Work Authority

Any crew member may immediately halt operations by calling:

**"STOP"**

Operations stop immediately. No argument. No delay. The issue is corrected before proceeding.

#### Conditions Requiring Stop Work

Call "STOP" if any of the following unsafe conditions exist:

- **Ladder Down**: Dive ladder is not raised and secured.
- **Current Line Deployed**: Current line is still in the water or not properly secured.
- **Diver Unaccounted For**: Any diver or passenger cannot be confirmed present.
- **Gear Unsecured**: Any dive gear, tanks, or equipment is not properly secured.
- **Passenger Unsafe**: Any guest is in an unsafe position or not seated properly.
- **Vessel Conflict**: Nearby vessel traffic creates a collision risk or departure path is blocked.
- **Other Hazard**: Any condition that poses risk to personnel, vessel, or equipment.

!!! danger
    Stop Work Authority is absolute. All crew are empowered and obligated to halt operations immediately when an unsafe condition is observed.

## Key Safety Rule

!!! danger
    No dock line, mooring line, anchor line, or other securing line shall be released without a direct command from the captain.

This rule applies at all times, in all conditions, for all vessel movements. Premature line release is a leading cause of vessel collisions, equipment damage, and diver injury. When in doubt, confirm with the captain before acting.

## Accountability

All crew members and captains are responsible for adhering to this SOP. Violations of the key safety rule or failure to use the challenge-and-response system will result in disciplinary action. Any incidents involving premature line release must be reported to management immediately.
